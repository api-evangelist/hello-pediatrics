# Hello Pediatrics

> Hello Pediatrics is a virtual pediatric medical practice that partners with primary care
> pediatric offices to cover their patients after hours. Board-certified pediatricians deliver
> telehealth triage, video visits and real-time messaging on nights, weekends and holidays, and
> send a visit summary back to the child's regular pediatrician. Hello Pediatrics publishes no
> public API, SDK, webhook surface or machine-readable API contract.

## No developer surface

- Probed 2026-08-22. No OpenAPI, Swagger, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server, A2A agent card,
  llms.txt, Postman collection, SDK or developer portal is published on any host this company
  controls. There is no api., developer. or docs. subdomain (all fail DNS resolution).
- Patient scheduling and video are delivered on a white-labeled third-party telehealth platform
  (Mend) at book.hellopediatrics.com. Any API behind that booking flow belongs to the platform
  vendor, not to Hello Pediatrics, and is not documented publicly under this brand.
- Partner practices receive visit summaries as sent reports, not through a published integration
  or EHR API — the partnerships page documents no data-exchange interface.
